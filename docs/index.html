Robotic Hand-Eye Calibration

AUTOHIAM leverages handeye_calibration_ros2 package to calibrate the RealSense D435 camera on the PiPER arm.


Motion Planning

AUTOHIAM leverages cuRobo for parallelized collision-free minimum-jerk robot motion generation.

Vision-based sample handling

AUTOHIAM leverages perception-driven control for sample transfering among different chemical solutions.

Even under disturbances, AUTOHIAM can accurately detect the position of the sample container and handle it for transfering accordingly.

Low-cost peristaltic pump system for automatic liquid handling

LBR iiwa 14 held a marker with pre-defined motion (straight line and Imperial 'I'), while iiwa 7 tracked the motion. Result demonstrated the robot's ability to follow both simple and complex patterns, with short and smooth paths.
